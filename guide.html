Comprehensive Guide to Stitch AI

Learn how our AI-powered lost and found platform connects missing items with their rightful owners using visual and descriptive matching.

1

Upload & Describe Your Item

Start by providing details about your lost item. You can either upload a photo or write a detailed description. Our system supports various categories such as outerwear, topwear, bottomwear, and footwear. The more specific you are about the brand, size, color, and distinguishing features, the more accurate the AI matching will be.

2

AI Processing & Matching

Once you hit the "Run AI Match Search" button, Stitch AI analyzes your input. The visual recognition algorithm identifies key characteristics from photos, while Natural Language Processing (NLP) parses your text description. It then cross-references this data with our Supabase-powered backend database of found items across multiple locations in real-time.

3

Review Matches & Secure Recovery

The system will display a list of potential matches ranked by confidence score. If you spot your item, you can initiate a secure recovery request. You will be connected with the individual or facility that reported the found item to coordinate its safe return.
