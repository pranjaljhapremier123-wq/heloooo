How Stitch AI Works

Stitch AI is an intelligent lost clothing finder designed to quickly connect people who have lost garments with found inventories using advanced description and tracking.

1. Describe Your Item

Enter precise details like category, brand, colour, location lost, and date to build a comprehensive profile of your missing garment.

2. Secure Storage

Your report is securely logged into our Supabase backend database (`lost_items`) instantly for real-time tracking.

3. Find & Match

Browse recent matches or let our system cross-reference listings to help you recover your clothing efficiently.
